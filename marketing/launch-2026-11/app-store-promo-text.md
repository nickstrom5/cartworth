# App Store Promotional Text for Thanksgiving

Promotional Text can be changed in App Store Connect at any time without a new build or review.
Limit: 170 characters. Changing it is the owner's action in App Store Connect.

## Use from now to 26 Nov

```
Thanksgiving is Nov 26. Price the whole list, turkey to pie crust, at every store near you, and read each turkey deal's conditions before you plan around it.
```

157 characters.

Why this one: it names the date, it describes two things the app really does (prices a list at
each store; shows weekly-ad conditions), and it promises no saving. "Every store near you" is the
phrase the live listing already uses.
