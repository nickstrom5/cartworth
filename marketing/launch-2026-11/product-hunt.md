- Posting your own product is allowed and encouraged; no outside "hunter" is needed.
- Paying anyone to hunt it is against the guidelines.

  upvote swaps and giveaways get launches penalised.

  final word.

**Maker's first comment:**

```
Hi, I'm Nick and I make Cartworth.

I built it because the sticker price is the wrong number to compare. A 12 oz box at $2.99 looks cheaper than a 42 oz canister at $9.49 and costs more per pound. Cartworth takes one search, prices it at the grocery stores near your ZIP at the same time, and puts every result in the same unit.

Three things I care about in it:

1. Every price says what kind it is: a shelf price, an online listing (which can run above the shelf), a chain-wide price, or a weekly-ad price. It never blends them, and when the two best prices are different kinds it won't put a number on the gap.
2. Weekly ads are shown with their conditions: limit 1, with card, must buy 2, with a $25 purchase.
3. No account, no sign-up, no analytics. There is nothing to sign up for.

The web app is free with no search limit, and it has a Thanksgiving list right now: add your sizes and it plans the trip across one, two or three stores. The iPhone app adds alerts, location and recipe photos; it has a free tier (three searches a day) and Pro at $3.99 a month, $19.99 a year or $29.99 once.

What it can't do: tell you how much you'll save. That depends on what you buy and where you live, and anyone who gives you a number is guessing. Published prices can also be wrong, so check the shelf tag.

It is US only. If it gets a match or a unit wrong in your ZIP, tell me the ZIP and the search and I'll fix it.

Independent. Not affiliated with any store.
```
