These are the brand's own accounts, so self-promotion is the point; the limits are the platforms'
spam rules (no duplicate posts across several accounts, no mass tagging, no reply-spam) and

the repo does not record whether the accounts exist. If they don't, the owner's personal accounts
work, with "I make Cartworth" in the post.

(`compliance.md` rule 31).

post below is under 280 with its link counted at X's fixed 23 characters, and under 300 with the
link written out in full, so one text works on all three. No hashtags beyond one, no store names, no prices.
