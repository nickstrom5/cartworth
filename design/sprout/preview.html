Sprout design system

Every token and component, light and dark side by side. Fonts are shown in their fallbacks until the Baloo 2 and DM Sans files are approved and added under /fonts/. The hero and the price rows switch between their phone and desktop layouts on the width of their own container, so a panel narrower than 40rem shows the phone layout. All prices on this page are layout samples.
