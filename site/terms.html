What Cartworth is

Cartworth is an information tool. It reads prices that retailers publish online — on their own sites where they publish one, and otherwise through the online storefront platform most chains use to publish their catalogue — together with the weekly circulars distributed on their behalf. It converts those prices to a common unit so that different package sizes can be compared, and presents the result alongside the distance to each store.

Cartworth is not a retailer, a marketplace, a delivery service, a broker or an agent. You cannot buy groceries through Cartworth, and no purchase you make at a store is a transaction with us. Cartworth does not set any retailer's prices and has no ability to influence them.

Accuracy of prices

This section is the heart of the agreement, so it is written in plain words.

  • Prices change without notice. A retailer can change a price between the moment it publishes it and the moment you reach the shelf. Nothing Cartworth shows you is a quote, an offer, or a price we can hold for you.
  • Published listings can be wrong. Retailers make mistakes in their own catalogues and circulars. Cartworth repeats what the retailer published; it cannot detect an error the retailer has not corrected.
  • Online prices are often not shelf prices. Where a retailer publishes only an online, delivery or pickup listing, that price can run above the in-store price, and retailers commonly say so themselves. Cartworth labels which kind of price you are looking at. Do not read an online price as a shelf price.
  • Weekly ad conditions apply. Multi-buy prices, BOGO offers, loyalty card prices and digital coupon prices carry conditions set by the retailer: purchase minimums, limits, date ranges, membership requirements and coupons you must clip. Cartworth surfaces those conditions where the circular states them, but the retailer's own terms govern the offer.
  • Unit prices can involve estimates. Where an item is sold by the piece and no weight is published, a typical weight may be used to produce a comparable unit price. Those figures are marked as estimates and should be treated as such.
  • Availability is not promised. Cartworth does not know whether an item is on the shelf at your store right now.
  • No savings are promised. We make no claim, express or implied, that using Cartworth will save you any particular amount of money, or any money at all.

Always check the shelf tag. Your decision to buy is yours, made in the store, on the basis of the price the store is actually charging.
