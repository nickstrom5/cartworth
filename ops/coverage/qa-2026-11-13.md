# QA, 13 Nov 2026 (Chicago 60618, Detroit 48201; live app after PR #23/#24)

Thanksgiving-list searches on app.cartworth.app (stuffing, heavy cream, pie crust, chicken broth,
celery) and three full runs of the test suite.

## Fixed

1. **The ads strip showed the same deal twice.** ALDI's circular prints some deals on two pages:
   "CHEF'S CUPBOARD Assorted Stuffing" $0.79, "FRIENDLY FARMS Heavy Whipping Cream" $4.59,
   "BAKE HOUSE CREATIONS Pie Crusts" $1.89 each appeared twice under "In this week's ads". Flipp's
   search returns both page items (same flyer 8221120, different item ids); the Deals tab already
   kept one, the search strip did not. Most searches that day had one such pair (turkey, cranberry
   sauce, butter too). The strip now uses the Deals tab's rule (store + name + price), on the web
   (`src/search.js adsFromHits`, `src/deals.js dedupeDeals`) and iPhone (`PriceSearch.uniqueAds`).
   The web strip also leaves out next week's circular now, as the iPhone already did.
2. **A flaky test was a real flash on cartworth.app.** "site axe: / at 390px" failed about one
   full run in three (serious color-contrast on the App Store button label). `appstore.js` turns
   the grey "Coming to the App Store" button green after load; `.btn` fades the background over
   0.14s while the label turns white at once, so for that moment the page showed white on
   #f3f6f4. A visitor whose page drew the grey button first (slow network) saw it too. The switch
   now happens with the transition off. `test/qa-appstore-live-2026-11-13.test.js` serves
   appstore.js 400 ms late and fails on the old code.
