## Fixed in this branch, most visible first

1. **Deals showed made-up percentages on per-pound prices with a per-pack saving.** Rouses' "Chicken
   Leg Quarters", $0.58/lb, "SAVE $2.19 PER PACK", read **79% off, Reg. $2.77**; the 10 lb pack is
   really $7.99 down to $5.80, 27%. Its shrimp, $4.99/lb, "SAVE $10.03 PER BAG", read 67% for 33%.
   Flipp's own `original_price` is the two numbers added. Now no regular price or percentage is
   shown for that shape (the story still says what is saved). `src/adapters/flipp.js`,
   `ios/App/Sources/FlippSource.swift`.
2. **A listing whose URL names another product could be the best price.** King Soopers' "KRO TURKEY
   STUFFING MIX" carried a pretzel listing's slug, 20 oz and $1.09, and was Denver's best stuffing at
   **$0.87/lb**. Whole Foods had two turkey rows linking to chicken products. Such a row is now a
   contradiction like any other (listed, "size unclear", never crowned). Over 1,981 captured rows the
   check flags those 4 and 3 renames (heirloom pumpkin, jewel yams), which only lose the crown.
   `src/normalize.js` and `ios/App/Core/ProductFactory.swift` (`slugProductConflict`).
3. **"milk" showed canned coconut milk as a milk deal.** Rouses Coconut Milk (13.5 oz, 2 for $4)
   and Carnation Evaporated Milk scored 0.51: under the results' 0.55 but over the ads strip's 0.4.
   A plant or canned base right before a dairy noun is now a strong penalty. Raising the ads cut
   instead would have hidden real ads (organic eggs, frozen green beans, Rhodes dinner rolls), which
   stay. `shared/match.js`, `ios/App/Core/Match.swift`.
4. **"oat milk" rejected Oatly** (0.28): Oatly is on the vegan-brand list, and a vegan analogue was
   penalised unless the query named a diet. A plant base in the query now counts. One-word "Oatmilk"
   (Oatly, Planet Oat) scored 0; it now reads as "oat milk".
5. **iPhone deal cards: the "% off" badge was unreadable** over the ad picture (18% tint, red text on
   the ad's red "SAVE"). It now has an opaque backing. The web badge was already opaque.
6. **cartworth.app's Thanksgiving guide said "The iPhone app is on its way"**, three days after launch.
   It now links the App Store listing (`site/tools/make-guides.py` and the generated page).
7. **A flaky test:** "a Kroger answer with a max-age is kept and reused" failed on any rerun within two
   minutes (the answer was still on disk). It now uses a query of its own each run.

## Not fixed, for the owner

- **"≈ same" covers almost everything when the winner is a chain-wide price.** "milk" in New Orleans:
  Walmart's national $3.86/gal wins, and ALDI, Sam's, Costco and Rouses up to **$5.44** are all
  "≈ same" (national is ±25%, Instacart up to 20% over shelf). By the stated rules this is right, but
  the chart stops telling stores apart. Options: narrow the national spread, or crown the best
  local price and show the national one beside it.
- **Onboarding's three store counts disagree** (iPhone, New Orleans): "Finding prices at 12 stores",
  "10 stores within 15 miles", "Start comparing 11 stores". Probably ad-only and no-price stores
  counted differently; one number, or a word on what each counts, would read better.
- **Suspect single picks:** Detroit "celery" is Kroger (Instacart) "Celery Sticks", 1 lb at **$0.29**,
  the URL agrees with the name, so nothing here can call it wrong; New Orleans "celery" is ALDI
  organic celery hearts per each ($2.59/ct), while other cities price celery per pound.
- KY's run logged a 429 from one retailer but still priced 11 stores.
